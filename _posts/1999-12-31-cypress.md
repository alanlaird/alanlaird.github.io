Big Cypress Seminole Indian Reservation, FL — New Year's Eve 1999

Seven and a half hours of music. Midnight fireworks. Sunrise over the swamp.

---

Runaway Jim, Funky Bitch, Tube, I Didn't Know, Punch You in the Eye,
Bouncing Around the Room, Poor Heart, Roggae, Split Open and Melt → Catapult,
Back on the Train, Horn, Guyute, After Midnight

Meatstick → Auld Lang Syne, Down with Disease → Llama, Bathtub Gin,
Heavy Things, Twist → Prince Caspian → Rock and Roll, You Enjoy Myself,
Crosseyed and Painless, The Inlaw Josie Wales, Sand → Quadrophonic Toppling,
Slave to the Traffic Light, Albuquerque, Reba, Axilla, Uncle Pen, David Bowie,
My Soul, Drowned → After Midnight (reprise), The Horse → Silent in the Morning →
Bittersweet Motel, Piper → Free, Lawn Boy, Hold Your Head Up → Love You →
Hold Your Head Up, Roses Are Free, Bug, Also Sprach Zarathustra →
Wading in the Velvet Sea → Meatstick
